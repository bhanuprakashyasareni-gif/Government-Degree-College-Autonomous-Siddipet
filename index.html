About the College

Government Degree College (Autonomous), Siddipet, established in 1956, is spread over an 18.29 acre campus. Affiliated to Osmania University, it offers 30+ UG and PG courses across Science, Arts, and Commerce. Accredited by NAAC, the college is known for academic excellence, modern labs, and a rich heritage.

Mission: Empowering students through knowledge, innovation, and ethics.

Vision: To be a centre of excellence in higher education and community engagement.

"We nurture critical thinking, research aptitude, and character. Every student is supported to realise their potential."

Dr. G. Sunitha
Principal, GDC (A) Siddipet

Admissions 2025-26

🧑‍🎓 UG (B.A / B.Com / B.Sc)

Through DOST (Telangana)

  • Register on DOST website
  • Fill personal & academic details
  • Choose college & course (web options)
  • Seat allotment (merit based – Intermediate marks)
  • Report to college for confirmation

Eligibility: Pass in 10+2 (any stream)

Fee (approx): ₹3,000 – ₹10,000 per year

🎓 PG (M.A / M.Com / M.Sc)

Through CPGET (Common PG Entrance Test)

  • Apply for CPGET, write entrance exam
  • Attend counselling, choose this college
  • Seat allotment based on rank

Eligibility: Relevant Bachelor’s degree

Fee (approx): ₹20,000 – ₹35,000 total

Documents required: SSC memo, Intermediate memo, Transfer Certificate, Aadhaar, Caste/Income (if applicable), photos.

Typical schedule: UG (DOST) May–July, PG (CPGET) June–August.

Reservation: As per Telangana government (SC/ST/BC/EWS, NCC/Sports/PH).
